reading: the TT bond rank is the EXACT interaction order along each cut — it is the
single number that says how far a function is from separable. Separable ⇒ all 1 ⇒ the
coefficient tensor is a pure outer product of per-axis vectors (place n vectors, done).
A rank-r bond means r outer-product terms are needed across that cut. For the INVERSE
method this is the design dial: pick the bond-rank profile you want, fill r·k per core,
and you have a function of prescribed interaction structure with NO reference function.
